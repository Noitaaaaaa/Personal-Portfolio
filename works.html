Nakachi Tracker

Deployed

A full-featured desktop logistics management system built for a real trucking company. Handles bookings, fleet, drivers, income, and expenses — all in one place. Built with a real client, deployed in production, and still in active use.

Tech Stack
Java SQLite Swing GUI JDBC Desktop App
↗ View Details

The Problem

The trucking company tracked all operations on paper — no visibility into income, expenses, or fleet status in real time. Everything was scattered across documents and notebooks.

What Was Built

🧭
Dashboard
Comprehensive overview of all logistics operations in real time.
👤
Profiles
Owners, clients, helpers, and drivers can view personal info and history.
🚚
Fleet & Maintenance
Manage the truck fleet and maintenance schedules in one view.
📁
Reports
Compiled logistics reports pulled directly from live system data.

Outcome

Forecasting entire operations and tracking income vs expenses became significantly easier. The client confirmed the system improved coordination and real-time visibility across the company.

Client Testimonial

"The program is very helpful to us. It tracks everything from our booking, our people, our income and our expenses. For a company who has several truck owners it helped us to see what is going-on real time."

— Susan Gajitos, Operations Manager, Nakachi Truck Rentals

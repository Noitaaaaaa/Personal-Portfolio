App Privacy

JourniBites.

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 2025

Overview

JourniBites is a personal food journal app for Android. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored. Your privacy is taken seriously — the app is built for personal use and does not collect or share your data with third parties.

Data We Access

JourniBites may request access to the following on your device:

  • Camera — to take photos of food and restaurants for your journal entries
  • Photo library / storage — to attach existing photos to entries
  • Location (GPS) — to log where you ate and display places on a map

These permissions are only used when you actively use those features. You can deny them without affecting the core journaling functionality.

How Data Is Stored

All data you create in JourniBites — journal entries, photos, ratings, notes, and location records — is stored locally on your device using SQLite. Nothing is uploaded to external servers or cloud services unless you explicitly enable a sync feature in a future version.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transmit your personal data to any third party. JourniBites does not contain ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking of any kind.

Data Deletion

You can delete any journal entry, photo, or record at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

JourniBites is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

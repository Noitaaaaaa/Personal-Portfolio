Nakachi Tracker Completed

A full featured desktop logistics management system for trucking application.

Java SQLite Desktop App

The Problem

The truck company had a problem with tracking and managing their logistics operations. This app was built to compress all of that work into one application, where everything will be seen clearly without having to skim through papers.

What Was Built

A desktop application wherein all operations are streamlined and managed in one place.

🧭
Dashboard
Provides a comprehensive overview of all logistics operations.
👤
Profiles
Allows Owners, Clients, Helpers and Drivers to check their personal information and history.
🚚
Trucks and Maintenance
Allows users to manage their fleet and maintenance schedules.
📁
Reports
Allows users to view various logistics reports compiled from the data in the system.

Outcome

Foreseeing entire operations and seeing where and how the income and expenses are distributed became easier.
